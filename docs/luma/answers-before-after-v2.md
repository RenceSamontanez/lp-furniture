# Luma answer format v2: before / after

Every built-in answer and every hand-seeded answer whose wording changed. Facts are the same, except for these **corrected** hand-seeded answers, which repeated things that are no longer true in the app (the new text uses the same live values as the built-in answers): *payment* (GCash "scan and upload your receipt" is gone: GCash goes through PayMongo), *installment* (the old "7 days, 15 days, 1, 2, 3 or 6 months, approved within 24 hours" is now the real plan: a down payment and the rest split by InstallmentScheduler), *delivery* (the "flat fee of 500 pesos" and "Mon to Sat" are gone: the shop sets the fee, hours come from config), *location* (hours now come from config, Monday to Friday) and *wood* (Narra, Lawaan and Gemelina became Gmelina, Mahogany, Acacia and Special request, as in the Customize page). Generated by comparing `database/data/luma-previous-defaults.json` against the current code; the Tagalog answer of each built-in intent was reworded the same way and is in the code.
