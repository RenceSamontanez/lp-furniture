Launcher and dismissing her

Luma's face replaces the speech-bubble icon. The small X hides her; the Assistant link in the menu brings her back.

Readable first

Message bubbles are solid, never see-through. Body text 16px, text contrast above 4.5:1 in light and dark.

Glass only as an accent

Blur appears on the header edge, the price badge and the launcher label. If the browser can't blur, they turn solid.

One clear action

The first button of a reply is solid; the others are outlined. Quick replies are lighter pills. Nothing competes.

Calm motion

Short fades and a sparkle that pulses while she thinks. All motion stops for people who prefer reduced motion.
