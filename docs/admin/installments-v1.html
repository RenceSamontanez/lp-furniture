Installment Terms

  • Down payment. 50% of the order is charged right away. The rest is split into equal installments. No interest, no late fees.
  • Automatic card charges. We charge your saved card on each due date through PayMongo. We never store your card number. GCash installments get a Pay Now button instead.
  • Reminders. A notification 3 days before every due date and a receipt after every payment.
  • Paying early. You can pay any installment, or the whole balance, early at any time.
  • Late payments. A declined charge is retried the next day, then you get a Pay Now link. After 3 days unpaid, it becomes Overdue.
  • Installment Standing. Late or overdue payments lower your standing score and can limit or remove installments on future orders. On-time payments raise it.
