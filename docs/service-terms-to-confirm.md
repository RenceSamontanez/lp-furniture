# Other Services terms: what the owner or a lawyer must confirm before launch

The page is `/services/terms` (`resources/views/services/terms.blade.php`). It is separate from the main Terms and Conditions (version 2), which were not edited, and it does not touch terms acceptance for furniture orders. The Other Services request form has a required checkbox and stores `terms_accepted_at` on the ticket (existing tickets are unaffected).

Everything below was decided with the owner and is written into the page and the code. None of it has been reviewed by a lawyer.

1. **No refund once work starts.** The page says the work is non-refundable once the laborer starts. Confirm the wording is enforceable for your customers and your area (consumer protection rules, distance selling rules).
2. **What counts as "started".** The system knows work has started only when an admin taps "Work started" (or "Service done"). Confirm the staff will tap it the moment the laborer begins, because the refund rules depend on it. A customer who asks for a refund a day after the laborer began, but before the admin tapped the button, would be offered a normal refund.
3. **Full refund.** If approved before work starts, the customer gets back the full amount paid. Confirm there is no deduction for a payment gateway fee (Stripe and PayMongo may not return their fee to you).
4. **Refund methods.** GCash or cash only, chosen by the customer, even if they paid by card. Confirm you accept a card payment being refunded outside the card.
5. **Seven days.** "Up to 7 days after approval." Confirm this is realistic and whether weekends and holidays count. The system highlights overdue refunds in the admin list.
6. **Wrong GCash number.** The page says the customer is asked to send the right number and that the 7 days start again. Confirm who bears the cost of money sent to a wrong number that the customer typed.
7. **Requests after work starts.** Reviewed individually, may be declined with a written reason, or approved as an exception. The exception option exists in the admin screen ("Approve as an exception", reason required, flagged and logged). The owner may ask for it to be removed.
8. **Cash refunds.** The customer picks the cash up from the shop. Confirm the collection rules (ID, who may collect, how long it is held) and whether a receipt is signed.
9. **Part payments.** The first half is also refunded in full if work has not started. Confirm that is what you want for the half-and-half plan.
10. **Nothing paid.** A customer who has paid nothing and whose work has not started is cancelled at once, with no approval. Confirm this is acceptable.
11. **Data.** The refund request stores the customer's phone, email and GCash number (encrypted at rest, shown masked in lists, never in logs). Confirm the privacy notice covers this and how long it is kept.
12. **Taxes.** This page is not tax advice. Refund and cancellation of an invoice affects your records after BIR registration; see `docs/service-invoices-to-confirm.md`.

## Stage 21h additions

13. **No pay, no work.** The admin can start work only after the customer paid: pay in full means the whole invoice, half and half means the first half (cash recorded by the admin counts the same). Confirm this is the rule you want, including for repeat customers you trust. Tickets started before this rule existed were left alone and show "Started before payments were required".
14. **Half and half for every service type.** From an invoice total of 3,000 pesos, for Labor only, Repair or restoration, Something else and any future type. Below that, full payment only. The threshold is one constant (`InvoicePaymentRules::HALF_HALF_MIN_TOTAL`).
15. **GCash reference numbers (10 to 13 digits).** "Mark refund as sent" accepts a GCash reference of 10 to 13 digits (spaces and dashes are ignored, the digits are stored). **This rule came from a third-party source, not from GCash's own documentation.** Please check it against a real GCash receipt. If a real reference does not match, the admin can still continue by ticking "This reference does not look like a normal GCash number" and writing a reason of at least 10 characters; the override and reason are recorded in the refund attempt and the ticket activity log (admin side only, never shown to the customer). Cash refunds need a receipt or voucher number of 4 to 20 letters, digits or dashes.
16. **The override is not shown in the request's message thread.** That thread is visible to the customer and has no admin-only lines, so the override is kept in the "Refund attempts" table on the admin request page and the ticket notes instead.

## Stage 21i additions (Luma, ticket quality)

17. **Visit and pickup fees.** Luma tells customers the work can be done at their home or at the shop, and that any visit or pickup fee is confirmed by the team and written on the invoice. She never states an amount. The invoice has "Home service fee" and one-way / round-trip "Delivery fee" lines. Please decide whether there is a standard fee, a fee by distance, or a case-by-case fee, and who may set it. Until then Luma only says "the team confirms it".
18. **Vague tickets.** A ticket now needs either a written description of at least 40 characters or a reference photo (up to 3). Luma coaches the customer (what is wrong, where, the size, the material, since when, a photo). If a ticket reaches the admin with neither, "Send to customer" shows an Important notice and needs the tick "I have enough details to do this work". What should staff do with a vague ticket? Call the customer first, decline it, or invoice anyway? Please also say how long a ticket may wait for more details before it is closed.
19. **Who sets target dates.** The owner said Other Services takes as long as the job needs and the admin sets the target per ticket. **The ticket has no target date field**, so Luma cannot read one: she says "our team will tell you the schedule" and never promises a date. Please decide whether to add a target date to the ticket (who sets it, whether the customer sees it, what happens when it is missed). The "taking too long" answer already reads the real work state and offers the cancellation request only while work has not started.
20. **"Where should the work be done?"** The customer chooses At my home / At your shop / Not sure. It is stored (`service_inquiries.work_location`) and shown to the admin; the team still confirms the place by phone. Confirm that is what you want, and whether a home visit needs an address on the ticket.
21. **Reference photos.** Photos are kept on the private disk and shown only to admins. Confirm how long they are kept and whether the privacy notice covers them.
22. **Luma and refunds.** Luma sends a request on the customer's behalf only after they tick "I checked these details", and never says a refund is approved, promised or guaranteed. She quotes the 7 days from the code constant. The customer may also ask Luma "where is my refund": she reads the real request (status, amount, method, due date, transaction number once sent).
